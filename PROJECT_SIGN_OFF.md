## PROJECT OVERVIEW

The Fuel Management System (FDMS) is a custom-built enterprise application designed specifically for Barki Traders to manage fuel inventory, accounting operations, customer and supplier transactions, financial reporting, and business operations through an integrated double-entry accounting system.

The application has been designed to improve operational efficiency, financial accuracy, inventory control, and reporting while maintaining complete auditability of all financial transactions.
